## Primary shipped gameplay promise in this source

`better_mining_lamp:better_mining_lamp` is a craftable copper utility helmet (165 durability, one defense) usable in the armor head slot or a Curios head slot. The client registers a water-sensitive, luminance-15 player dynamic light **only if** the optional Sodium Dynamic Lights mod is loaded; without that provider, do not promise actual moving light. The bundled dynamic-light item definition also advertises luminance 15 and water sensitivity. Server-side Curios-head wear is at least one durability per positive, non-armor-bypassing final damage event, otherwise `floor(damage / 4)`; exhaustion breaks the stack. These are source contracts, not observations of real multiplayer clients. See `MiningHelmetItem`, `MiningLampLightPolicy`, `MiningHelmetEvents`, `MiningHelmetClient`, `DynamicLightsCompat`, the dynamiclights JSON and `META-INF/mods.toml`.

**Acquisition roots:** `src/main/resources/data/better_mining_lamp/recipes/better_mining_lamp.json` defines a shaped recipe (`" L " / "CCC" / "C C"`): one vanilla lantern and five vanilla copper ingots produce the lamp. `MiningHelmetMod` registers the item and adds it to Tools & Utilities. `data/curios/tags/items/head.json` lists it as head Curio; `data/minecraft/tags/items/repairs_copper_utility_armor.json` lists copper ingots for repairs, and `MiningHelmetItem.isValidRepairItem` accepts them. Qualification must confirm the **full-pack** recipe is not replaced/removed, lantern and copper remain practically obtainable in the selected world, and normal survival crafting/repair and Curios head equipping work; registry existence alone does not establish those facts. There is no block placed by this mod, no authored worldgen, and no mod-specific GUI in these sources; Curios inventory/slots and optional lighting are external integrations.
